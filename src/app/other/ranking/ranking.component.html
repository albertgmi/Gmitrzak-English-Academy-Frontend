📊 How scoring works
Score = (Period Score + Activity Points) × 0.7 + Avg. Grade × 0.3
Period Score (0–100)
  • 30% Homework done on time
  • 20% Lesson attendance (2/week = full)
  • 15% Watched assigned videos
  • 20% Flashcard regularity (3+ days)
  • 15% Flashcards vs target
Activity Points

Earned instantly for daily activity — flashcards, sentences, memories, pronunciation, essays and completed assignments. Added directly on top of your score.

Average Grade

Your average grade percentage for the selected period — contributes 30% to your final score.

💡 Activity Points have no upper limit — the more consistent you are every day, the higher you climb.
